AI Transparency Statement

I use AI tools while working on this site. I use them to explore ideas, organize and edit drafts, refine wording, and help build and revise the site. I also use generative tools to develop visual material, including the hero illustration on the homepage.

The questions I pursue, the direction of each essay, and the decisions about what to publish are mine. AI tools can suggest language, structure, code, or images; I review and revise their contributions before publishing. That review is not a guarantee that every detail is correct, so I welcome corrections.

The short version: the work is mine, and AI is one of the tools I use to make it. The distinction is sometimes less tidy in practice than that sentence suggests, which is why I want to be clear about the tools involved.
